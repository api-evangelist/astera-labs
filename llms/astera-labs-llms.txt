Redrivers: An Eye\-Popping Difference](https://www.asteralabs.com/pci-express-retimers-vs-redrivers-an-eye-popping-difference/): A redriver amplifies a signal, whereas a retimer retransmits a fresh copy of the signal\. Retimers provide capabilities such as PCIe® protocol participation, lane\-to\-lane skew compensation, adaptive EQ, diagnostics features, etc\.
